## Start with your next session

- **Plan your points.** Choose a class, spend talent points and copy a link to share both talent sets.
- **Check your group.** Fill raid slots, see buff coverage and share the same roster with your guild.
- **Explore the database.** Look up [items, NPCs and more](https://everloot.app/database). Player observations build the evidence; game-file data stays labelled until confirmed in play.
- **Help test the addon.** [Download and installation instructions](https://everloot.app/addon). Live-game verification is still pending; report what works and what breaks.

## Help make it better

Try a talent build or a raid roster, then tell us which step was confusing or which result looked wrong.
Share the page or build link and what you expected in [Discord](https://discord.gg/9CKsbHTRu3) or a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/Everloot/everloot/issues/new/choose).
Please leave passwords, account details and private game files out of public reports.

Read [how the database counts evidence](https://everloot.app/methodology), or
[contribute to the project](https://github.com/Everloot/everloot).
The main repository contains the addon, website and desktop companion, with screenshots and setup instructions.
